## Overview

The Reports page provides visual summaries of your financial data with filterable charts.

## Charts

All charts are pure SVG with no external library dependencies.

| Chart | Description |
|---|---|
| **Donut Chart** | Category spending breakdown |
| **In and Out** | Money in and out by month, as a chart or a table |
| **Top Expenses** | The largest expenses with total and share |
| **Net Worth Chart** | Net worth history line chart |
| **Budgets This Period** | Budget utilization bars |
| **Category Table** | Spending by category with progress bars |

## Filters

- **Period selector** — Last 30 days, 3 months, 6 months, 1 year, all time
- **Account filter** — Flat multi-select
- **Category filter** — Grouped by category group (housing, food, etc.) with group-level toggle. Selecting a group header toggles all categories in that group.

## Report Period

Reports use the `useDateRange` hook to compute start/end dates from the selected period string.
