Accounts are the places your money lives: bank accounts, cards, cash, investments and loans. Every transaction belongs to an account, and the dashboard totals are computed from them.

## Account Types

The **Type** field offers Checking, Savings, Credit Card, Cash, Investment, Loan and Other. You can also type your own type. The Accounts page groups accounts into the tabs Bank, Cards, Cash, Loans, Investments and Other, and a custom type falls under Other.

**Counts as** says whether the account is an **Asset** or a **Liability**. Picking a built-in type sets it for you (Credit Card and Loan are liabilities); you can change it. Net worth is the assets minus the liabilities.

## Creating an Account

1. Open **Accounts** from the sidebar.
2. Select **New Account**.
3. Enter a name, pick or type a type, choose a currency and enter the initial balance.
4. Select **Create Account**.

## Editing an Account

Open the account's menu and choose **Edit**. The balance field is then called **Opening Balance**: it is the balance before any transaction.

## Archiving and Deleting

An account's menu has **Archive** and **Delete**.

- **Archive** closes an account and keeps its history. An archived account leaves the totals and the account pickers, and appears under the **Archived** tab. An account can be archived only when its balance is 0; otherwise the app asks you to move the balance out first. **Unarchive** brings it back.
- **Delete** is only for an account with no transactions. If it has any, the app refuses, and Archive is the way to close it.

## Account Detail Page

Select an account to open its page. It shows a **Balance Over Time** chart, what came in and went out this month, and the account's transactions.

## Loans and People

A **Loan** account keeps track of money lent to or borrowed from people. When the account has transactions, a **By Person** card lists one row per person, with a balance:

- The person is read from the description. A description such as "Soldi Alice" ("Soldi" is Italian for "money") puts the transaction in the bucket **Alice**. Without that prefix, the whole description is the bucket's name.
- Above 0 they owe you. Below 0 you owe them. The card shows the totals in both directions.
- A typo makes a second bucket. Edit the description to merge them.

Select a name to see that person's transactions.

## How Balances Work

An account's balance is its initial balance adjusted by every transaction:

- **Income** increases it.
- **Expense** decreases it.
- **Transfers** decrease the source account and increase the destination account.

## Tips

- **Name accounts clearly** — "Everyday Checking" beats "Account 1".
- **Reconcile now and then** — compare the balance with your bank's.
