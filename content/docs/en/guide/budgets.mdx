---
title: Budgets
description: Set spending limits by category and track your progress.
---

A budget is a spending limit for a category over a month or a year. Alter Ego tracks what you have spent against it.

## Creating a Budget

1. Open **Budgets** from the sidebar.
2. Select **New Budget**.
3. Enter a **Name**, pick a **Category** and a **Limit**, and choose the **Period**: Monthly or Yearly.
4. Choose a **Rollover** mode (see below).
5. Select **Create Budget**.

## Rollover

The **Rollover** field has four options: **Reset each period**, **Carry surplus forward**, **Carry deficit forward** and **Full rollover**. The limit a budget is measured against can therefore differ from the amount you entered.

## Progress

Each budget card shows what has been spent against the limit, with a bar and a state:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| On track | Under 80% of the limit |
| Near limit | From 80% of the limit |
| Over | At or past the limit |

Budgets that are over their limit are listed first. A budget whose limit is zero or less counts as over as soon as anything is spent against it.

## Where Else Budgets Show Up

- The **Budgets** card on the Dashboard lists the five budgets nearest their limit.
- Opening the dashboard shows a toast for budgets over their limit and one for budgets at 80% or more, at most once a day.
- The **Budgets This Period** card in Reports draws each budget as a bar.

## Editing and Deleting

The actions menu on a budget card has **Edit** and **Delete**. Deleting a budget does not touch your transactions.

## Tips

- **Start with your biggest categories.**
- **Categorize consistently** — a budget only counts transactions filed under its category.
