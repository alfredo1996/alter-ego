title: Budgets
description: Set spending limits by category and track your progress.
---

Budgets help you control spending by setting a monthly limit for specific categories. Alter Ego tracks how much you've spent against each budget and alerts you when you're getting close.

## Creating a Budget

1. Go to the **Budgets** page from the sidebar.
2. Tap **Add Budget**.
3. Select a category (e.g., Groceries, Dining Out, Entertainment).
4. Enter your monthly spending limit.
5. Tap **Save**.

Each budget applies to a single category. You can create as many budgets as you like.

## Progress Tracking

Each budget card shows:

- **Category name and emoji** — so you can identify it at a glance.
- **Amount spent** — your total spending in that category for the current month.
- **Budget limit** — the cap you set.
- **Progress bar** — a visual indicator of how much of the budget you've used.

## Status Colors

The progress bar changes color based on how close you are to your limit:

| Status | Color | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| OK | Green | Under 80 % of your budget |
| Warning | Amber | Between 80 % and 99 % |
| Danger | Red | At or over 100 % |

## Dashboard Alerts

When any budget reaches the warning or danger threshold, it appears in the **Budgets at Risk** widget on your dashboard. You'll also see a toast notification once per day when you open the app.

These alerts help you catch overspending early so you can adjust before the month ends.

## Budgets in Reports

In the **Reports** page, the "Budgets This Period" card lists each budget with a progress bar of your spending so far, so you see where you're on track and where you've gone over.

## Editing and Deleting

Tap the three-dot menu on any budget card to edit the limit or delete the budget entirely. Deleting a budget removes the tracking — it does not affect the underlying transactions.

## Tips

- **Start broad** — if you're new to budgeting, create budgets for your three or four biggest spending categories first. You can always add more later.
- **Review monthly** — at the end of each month, check which budgets you hit and which you stayed under. Adjust limits to be realistic rather than aspirational.
- **Pair with reports** — use the Budgets This Period card in Reports to spot trends over multiple months.
- **Categorize consistently** — budgets rely on transaction categories. If spending is miscategorized, your budget tracking will be inaccurate.
