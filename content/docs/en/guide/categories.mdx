Categories say what a transaction was for. The app ships a default set, and you can change it or add your own.

## The Categories Page

Categories are shown in one card per group (Income, Housing, Transportation, Food, Utilities, Healthcare, Personal, Entertainment, Education, Savings, Debt and Other). The page has a search box and the tabs **All**, **Income** and **Expenses**. Each category shows how much was spent in it this month, and a category with subcategories lists them indented.

## Creating a Category

1. Select **New Category**.
2. Enter a **Name**, pick a **Group** and a **Color**.
3. Save.

## Editing, Resetting and Deleting

Each category has an actions menu:

- **Edit** changes its name, group or color. Editing a default category saves your own copy of it.
- **Reset to Default** appears on a default category you have edited, and drops your copy.
- **Delete** is only for categories you made, marked **Yours**. Transactions that used it become uncategorized.

To undo all of it at once, **Reset Categories to Default** in Settings, under Danger Zone, deletes your categories, undoes your edits to the defaults and brings back the default set.

## Tips

- **Keep the list short** — budgets and reports are easier to read with few categories.
