---
title: Recurring Transactions
description: Automate repeating income and expenses.
---

Recurring transactions represent regular financial events — your monthly rent, biweekly paycheck, annual subscriptions, or loan payments. Alter Ego automatically generates transactions based on the rules you define.

## Creating a Recurring Rule

1. Go to the **Recurring** page from the sidebar.
2. Tap **Add Recurring**.
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Description** — what this payment is for (e.g., "Netflix", "Rent", "Salary").
   - **Amount** — the transaction amount.
   - **Type** — expense, income, or transfer.
   - **Account** — which account this transaction affects.
   - **Category** — the spending or income category.
   - **Frequency** — how often it repeats: daily, weekly, biweekly, monthly, or yearly.
   - **Start date** — when the recurrence begins.
   - **End date** (optional) — when it should stop recurring.
4. Tap **Save**.

## How Auto-Generation Works

Each time you open the app, Alter Ego checks your recurring rules and generates any transactions that are due. For example, if you have a monthly rule starting January 1 and you open the app on March 5, it will generate transactions for January, February, and March if they haven't been created yet.

Generated transactions appear in your transaction list like any other transaction. You can edit or delete them individually without affecting the recurring rule.

## Pause and Resume

If a recurring transaction is temporarily on hold (e.g., a paused subscription), you can pause the rule instead of deleting it:

- Tap the **Pause** button on the recurring card.
- The rule stops generating new transactions until you resume it.
- Tap **Resume** when you want it to start again.

Paused rules appear in the "Paused" section of the Recurring page.

## Loan Payments

For loans and mortgages, recurring transactions support amortization:

- Rules that carry **loan metadata** (the interest rate and loan term, for example from an earlier import) are split this way.
- Each generated payment is automatically split into **principal** and **interest** portions based on an amortization schedule.
- The loan details appear as a badge on the recurring card (e.g., "4.5 % / 30 yr").

Rules with loan metadata are listed under the **Loans** tab.

## Sections on the Recurring Page

The Recurring page organizes your rules into sections:

- **Active** — rules that are currently generating transactions.
- **Loans** — active rules with loan metadata attached.
- **Paused** — rules you've temporarily stopped.

## Editing and Deleting

Tap the three-dot menu on any recurring card to edit or delete the rule. Editing a rule affects only future generated transactions — past transactions are unchanged.

## Tips

- **Set up all your fixed costs first** — rent, utilities, subscriptions, loan payments. This gives you an accurate picture of your committed spending.
- **Use end dates for temporary commitments** — if a subscription runs for 12 months, set an end date so it stops automatically.
- **Review generated transactions** — auto-generated transactions use the amount from the rule. If a bill varies month to month, you may need to adjust individual transactions after they're generated.
