---
title: Recurring Transactions
description: Automate repeating income and expenses.
---

A recurring rule describes a payment that repeats: rent, a salary, a subscription, a loan installment. Alter Ego creates the transactions that fall due.

## Creating a Rule

1. Open **Recurring** from the sidebar.
2. Select **New Rule**. The dialog is called **New Recurring Transaction**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Description**, **Type** and **Amount**.
   - **Account** and **Category**.
   - **Frequency** — Daily, Weekly, Monthly or Yearly — and **Every (interval)**, how many of those between occurrences.
   - **Start Date**, and optionally an **End Date**.
4. Save.

## How Due Transactions Are Created

The first time you open the Recurring page on a given day, the app creates the transactions that have come due. They appear in the Transactions list like any other, and you can edit or delete them without touching the rule.

## Pause and Resume

Each rule has a **Pause** button, which becomes **Resume** once paused. A paused rule is marked **Paused** and creates nothing until you resume it.

## The Tabs

- **Active** — rules that are running.
- **Loans** — rules that carry loan details. The tab appears only when you have one, and the rule shows a badge such as "Loan 4.5% · 30 yr".
- **Paused** — rules you have paused.
- **Suggested** — repeating charges the app found in the last 13 months that no rule covers yet. **Confirm** opens a new rule already filled in; a dismissed charge is not suggested again.

When rules are running, three figures sit above the tabs: **In every month**, **Out every month** and **Left every month**.

## Editing and Deleting

Each rule has **Edit** and **Delete** buttons.

## Tips

- **Start with fixed costs** — rent, utilities, subscriptions.
- **Use an End Date** for commitments that stop.
- **Check generated amounts** — a transaction uses the rule's amount, so adjust it if a bill varies.
