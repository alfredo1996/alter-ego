title: Transactions

Every time money moves, you record a transaction: a purchase, a paycheck, a transfer between your own accounts.

## Transaction Types

- **Expense** — money going out.
- **Income** — money coming in.
- **Transfer** — money moving between two of your accounts.

## Creating a Transaction

1. Select **New Transaction** in the header of the Dashboard, Accounts or Transactions page, or the **+** in the top bar on a phone.
2. Choose the type.
3. Fill in the amount, date, description, account and category. A transfer has a **From Account** and a **To Account** instead of a category. **Notes** are optional.
4. Save.

**Split** next to the category lets you divide one transaction between several categories; **Single category** goes back.

### Suggested Categories

While you type a description, the form can offer **Suggested:** followed by a category name. It comes from your auto-categorization rules in Settings: the first active rule whose pattern the description contains (ignoring case). Select the suggestion to apply it; nothing is filled in for you.

## Editing and Deleting

Select a transaction to edit it or delete it. Deleting a transaction adjusts the account balance.

## Swipe Gestures

On a phone you can swipe a row: left to delete, right to edit.

## Filtering

The Transactions page has a search box, the tabs **All**, **Income**, **Expenses** and **Transfers**, and these filters:

- **Dates** — a date range.
- **Account** and **Category**. The category list starts with **Uncategorized**.
- **Tags**.
- **More Filters** — **Status** (Pending, Cleared or Reconciled), **Min amount**, **Max amount** and **Has note**.

Filters are kept in the address, so a filtered view can be bookmarked.

## Selecting Several Transactions

Select rows to act on them together. The bar that appears can delete the selection, or change its account or date.

## Importing

The **More** menu in the header has the two importers.

### Import CSV

1. **Import Transactions** — choose an account and upload a CSV file.
2. **Map CSV Columns** — match the file's columns to the transaction fields. The importer suggests a mapping where it can.
3. **Review & Import** — check the parsed rows, then import. Rows that were already imported are skipped.

### Money Manager

**Money Manager** imports a CSV export from the Money Manager app, in four steps: Upload, Reconcile, Preview and Import. Reconcile lists the accounts and categories found in the file. Importing the same file twice is safe: duplicates are detected and skipped.

## Exporting

**Export…** in the **More** menu opens **Export Transactions**. Choose a date range and download a CSV. **Expand splits into separate rows** gives one row per split, with its own category and amount. For a backup of everything, use **Export All Data (JSON)** in Settings.

## Tips

- **Use the + button** on a phone to log a purchase the moment it happens.
- **Categorize everything** — the dashboard, budgets and reports depend on it.
- **Tag for context** — tags cut across categories, for example a trip.
