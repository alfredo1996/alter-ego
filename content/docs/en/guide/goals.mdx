title: Savings Goals
description: Set financial targets and track your progress toward them.
---

Savings goals let you set a target amount and deadline, then track your contributions over time. Whether you're saving for a vacation, an emergency fund, or a down payment, goals keep you motivated with clear progress indicators.

## Creating a Goal

1. Navigate to the **Goals** page from the sidebar (labeled "Target" in the nav).
2. Tap **Add Goal**.
3. Enter a name for your goal (e.g., "Emergency Fund").
4. Set the **target amount** — how much you want to save.
5. Optionally set a **deadline** — the date you'd like to reach your target by.
6. Enter the **current amount** — how much you've already saved toward this goal (can be zero).
7. Tap **Save**.

## Progress Tracking

Each goal card displays:

- **Progress bar** — a visual indicator of how far along you are.
- **Percentage complete** — your current amount as a percentage of the target.
- **Remaining amount** — how much more you need to save.
- **Days remaining** — if you set a deadline, how many days are left (not shown if no deadline is set).

## Adding Funds

As you save money, update your goal progress:

1. Tap the goal card to open it.
2. Use the **Add Funds** action.
3. Enter the amount you're contributing.
4. Tap **Save**.

The progress bar and percentage update immediately.

## Dashboard Widget

Your top three active goals appear in the **Savings Goals** widget on the dashboard. This gives you a quick glance at your progress without leaving the main screen. Tap the widget to navigate to the full Goals page.

## Completing a Goal

When your current amount reaches or exceeds your target, the progress bar fills to 100 %. There's no special "complete" action — the goal simply shows as fully funded. You can delete it when you no longer need to track it.

## Deleting a Goal

Tap the three-dot menu on a goal card and select **Delete**. You'll be asked to confirm. Deleting a goal removes it from tracking but doesn't affect any transactions or account balances.

## Tips

- **Be specific** — "Vacation to Japan — $3,000" is more motivating than "Travel Fund."
- **Set realistic deadlines** — divide the remaining amount by the days left to see how much you need to save daily. If the number feels too high, extend the deadline.
- **Use multiple goals** — there's no limit on how many goals you can have. Separate short-term goals (new phone) from long-term ones (house down payment).
- **Review on the dashboard** — the widget keeps your top goals visible so they stay top of mind.
