---
title: Savings Goals
description: Set targets and track your progress toward them.
---

A savings goal has a target amount and, optionally, a target date. You add money to it over time and the card shows how far along you are. The sidebar item is **Goals**.

## Creating a Goal

1. Open **Goals** from the sidebar.
2. Select **New Goal**.
3. Enter a **Goal name** and a **Target amount**, and choose a **Currency**.
4. Optionally set a **Target date**, an **Icon** and a **Color**.
5. Save.

## Progress

Each goal card shows:

- A state: **Saving**, **Overdue** (the target date has passed) or **Reached**.
- A progress bar, the percentage saved and the amount to go.
- The target date and the days left, or how many days it is overdue.
- With a target date more than a month away, the amount a month that gets you there on time.

## Adding Funds

Select **Add Funds** on a goal's card, enter an **Amount** and confirm. The bar updates at once.

## Reaching a Goal

When the saved amount reaches the target, the card says **Goal reached!** and shows **Reached**. Nothing else happens: delete the goal when you no longer want to see it. The page has the tabs **All**, **In Progress** and **Reached**.

## Editing and Deleting

The actions menu on a card has **Edit** and **Delete**. Deleting a goal does not touch your transactions or account balances.

## Tips

- **Be specific** with the name.
- **Set a target date** to see what a month needs to be set aside.
