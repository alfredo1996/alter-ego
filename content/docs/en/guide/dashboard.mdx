The dashboard is your home screen — a single-page summary of your entire financial picture. Everything here updates automatically as you add transactions, adjust budgets, or contribute to savings goals.

## At a Glance

At the top of the dashboard, four figures for the current month:

- **Total balance** — the sum of all your account balances.
- **This month in** — total of all income transactions this month.
- **This month out** — total of all expense transactions this month.
- **Savings rate** — the share of this month's income you kept.

## Accounts

Each of your accounts appears as a tile showing its name and current balance. Tap any tile to open the account detail page with a balance chart and filtered transaction list.

## Spending by Category

A donut and a ranked list of your top spending categories for the current month, with the amount spent in each. Tap any row to jump to the Transactions page filtered to that category.

## Budgets

The budgets that are approaching or exceeding their limit appear here:

- **Near limit** — you've spent 80 % or more of a budget.
- **Over limit** — you've hit or exceeded 100 %.

You'll also receive a daily toast notification the first time you open the dashboard if any budgets are at risk.

## Upcoming Bills

Your next recurring payments, soonest first.

## Goals

Your top active savings goals, each with a progress bar and the percentage complete. Tap the card to go to the Goals page.

## Recent Activity

The last few transactions across all accounts, showing the date, description, category, and amount. Tap **View all** to open the Transactions page.

## Uncategorized Transactions

If recent activity includes transactions without a category, a notice appears near the top of the dashboard. Tap **Categorize** to jump directly to the Transactions page filtered to uncategorized items so you can assign categories quickly.

## Tips

- **Drill down** — most elements on the dashboard are tappable. Use them to navigate to the relevant detail page instead of hunting through the sidebar.
- **Keep categories tidy** — the category spending breakdown and budget tracking both rely on accurate categorization. Use auto-categorization rules in Settings to reduce manual work.
- **Check daily** — the budget alert toast only fires once per day, so a quick morning glance keeps you on track.
