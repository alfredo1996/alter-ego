The dashboard is the home screen: a summary of the current month that updates as you add transactions, adjust budgets or contribute to savings goals. On a new account it shows **Get started with Alter Ego** and an **Add Your First Account** button.

## At a Glance

Four figures sit at the top:

- **Net worth** — your assets minus your liabilities, with both amounts underneath.
- **This month in** — income recorded this month.
- **This month out** — expenses recorded this month.
- **Savings rate** — the share of this month's income you kept.

## Accounts

Each account appears as a card with its balance. Select one to open its detail page.

## Spending by Category

A donut and a legend for the current month's spending, with the amount in each category. The legend names every category.

## Budgets, Upcoming Bills and Goals

These three cards appear only when you have something to show in them:

- **Budgets** — the five budgets nearest their limit, one bar each. The rest are on the Budgets page.
- **Upcoming Bills** — active recurring expenses and transfers due within the next 30 days, soonest first.
- **Goals** — your savings goals that are not yet reached, the furthest along first.

When you open the dashboard and a budget is over its limit, a toast names it; budgets at 80% or more of their limit are counted in another toast. Each browser shows them at most once a day.

## Recent Activity

The latest transactions across all accounts. **View all** opens the Transactions page.

## Uncategorized Transactions

If recent activity includes transactions without a category, a line near the top says how many. **Categorize** opens the Transactions page filtered to those.

## Tips

- **Select to drill down** — account cards, budgets, bills and goals each lead to their own page.
- **Keep categories tidy** — the spending card and the budgets both rely on categories.
