title: Reports
description: See where your money came from and where it went.
---

The Reports page summarizes a period: money in and out, spending by category, the largest expenses, budgets and net worth.

## Period

The **Period type** selector offers:

- **Monthly** (the default, for the current month) and **Yearly**. Both have a stepper to move to the previous or next month or year.
- **Last 12 months**, **Last 3 months**, **Last 6 months** and **Rolling 12 months**.
- **Custom range**, with a date picker.

The period is kept in the address, so a report can be bookmarked.

## Filters and Comparison

- **Categories** and **Accounts** narrow the report's figures, charts and lists, except **Net Worth** and **Budgets This Period**, which take neither the filters nor the selected period. Budgets always shows the current period.
- **Compare** appears for a month or a year: **Previous Month** or **Same Month Last Year** for a month, **Previous Year** for a year. The money in and out figures then show the difference.

## Sections

On a phone, a row of links at the top jumps between the sections.

### In and Out

**Money in**, **Money out** and **Net** for the period, beside a month-by-month chart. **Chart** and **Table** show the same numbers.

### Where It Went

A donut and a list of spending by category, with the amount and the share of each. Small categories are gathered under **Everything else**, which opens to show them.

### Top Expenses

The largest expenses of the period. **Show All** lists the rest.

### Budgets

Each budget as a bar, the fullest first. This section appears when you have budgets.

### Net Worth

Net worth at each month end, how far it moved since the month before, the line over time, and the assets and debts behind it. With a single snapshot there is no line yet.

## Subscriptions

Repeating charges that look like subscriptions are listed on the Recurring page, under **Suggested**. A link on Reports leads there.

## Tips

- **Check at month end** to see patterns early.
- **Use Compare** to see whether a month was heavier than the last.
