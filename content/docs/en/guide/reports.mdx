The Reports page gives you a visual breakdown of your finances over any time period. Use it to spot trends, compare categories, and understand where your money goes.

## Period Selector

At the top of the Reports page, choose the time window for all charts and statistics:

- Last 30 days
- Last 3 months
- Last 6 months (default)
- Last 12 months
- Year to date
- Custom date range

Changing the period updates every chart and stat on the page immediately.

## Summary Statistics

Four key figures displayed at the top:

- **Total Income** — all income transactions in the selected period.
- **Total Expenses** — all expense transactions in the selected period.
- **Net Savings** — income minus expenses.
- **Savings Rate** — net savings as a percentage of income.

## Category Donut Chart

A donut chart showing how your expenses break down by category. Each slice is color-coded and labeled with the category name and percentage. Hover or tap a slice to see the exact amount.

## Category Table

A detailed table listing every category with spending in the selected period:

- Category name and emoji.
- Total amount spent.
- Percentage of total expenses.
- A progress bar showing relative size compared to the top category.

Use this table when you need exact numbers rather than the visual overview the donut chart provides.

## In and Out

The top card shows the period's money in, money out and net, beside the months they sit in. Pick a month and it is drawn with the three months before it; a year or a range draws its own months. Switch between **Chart** and **Table** for the same numbers.

- **Green bars, with a +** — money in.
- **Dark bars, with a −** — money out.
- The net sits under each month.

## Top Expenses

The largest expenses of the period, with their total and share of all spending. **Show All** lists more.

## Budgets This Period

Each budget with its spending so far, as a progress bar that changes state as you near or pass the limit. It appears when you have budgets set up.

## Net Worth

Your net worth (assets minus debts) with how it moved since last month end, and a line over the period. Data points come from the daily snapshots the app records automatically, so the line fills in over time on a new account.

## Filters

You can narrow reports by:

- **Account** — show transactions from a specific account only.
- **Category** — focus on one or more categories.

Filters combine with the period selector to give you exactly the view you need.

## Tips

- **Check monthly** — reviewing reports at the end of each month helps you catch spending patterns before they become habits.
- **Compare periods** — switch between "Last 3 months" and "Last 6 months" to see if trends are improving or worsening.
- **Use with budgets** — the Budgets This Period card is most useful when you have budgets set up for your key categories.
- **Track net worth** — even if you don't use anything else here, watching your net worth trend upward over time is a powerful motivator.
